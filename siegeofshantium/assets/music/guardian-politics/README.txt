Siege of Shantium — Guardian Political Arc Music
=================================================

11 — THE FIRST SEAT
File: 11_the_first_seat.mid
Approx. duration: 101 sec
Use: One-time / rare threshold cue when the Guardian-aligned faction first obtains formal political control of any settlement.
Character: Historical, weighty, restrained triumph. Begins close to Guardian Hall's chamber warmth, then acquires civic brass, timpani, and measured snare. The ending deliberately remains morally open rather than becoming a coronation fanfare.

12 — BENEATH THE GUARDIAN STANDARD
File: 12_beneath_the_guardian_standard.mid
Approx. duration: 100 sec
Use: General settlement-control theme when visiting a settlement governed by a Guardian-aligned faction.
Character: Stable, civic, recognizably Guardian. Warm strings, cello, harp, oboe, restrained horn and very soft military pulse. Built as a loop.

13 — THE WEIGHT OF CHOICE
File: 13_the_weight_of_choice.mid
Approx. duration: 135 sec
Use: Introspective political-power scenes: legitimacy, responsibility, doubt, "am I doing the right thing?"
Character: Sparse slow strings, harp, cello and flute. The common Guardian political motif is fragmented and left unresolved. No percussion. Built as a loop.

14 — THE NECESSARY HAND
File: 14_the_necessary_hand.mid
Approx. duration: 117 sec
Use: Authoritarian-certainty state: the Guardian is convinced that imposing their judgment is necessary and opposition should get out of the way.
Character: The same Guardian motif is converted into hard unison brass over relentless low-string pulses, timpani and regimental snare. An oboe briefly recalls the older, more personal Guardian identity before being overwhelmed by the institutional sound. Built as a loop.

COMMON MUSICAL DNA
- All four are centered around a new Guardian political motif: D–F–A–G–E.
- Grounded medieval/cinematic orchestral palette: strings, harp, woodwinds, low brass and restrained military percussion.
- Instrumental only.
- "The First Seat" is an event cue; tracks 12–14 include LOOP_START / LOOP_END MIDI markers.
- Standard General MIDI; no proprietary soundfont dependencies.
