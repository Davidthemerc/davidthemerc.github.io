Numeric keypad training • v1.0.2

10-Key Academy

Dedicated numeric-entry training for keypad home position, vertical finger columns, zero and decimal control, Enter rhythm, accounting-style values, and a two-minute proficiency test. HyperSoft reports KPH separately from ordinary typing WPM.

Numeric Skills
Home row: 4 · 5 · 6Right index on 4, middle on 5, ring on 6. Use the tactile marker on 5 when your keypad has one.
Vertical finger columnsIndex 1/4/7 · Middle 2/5/8 · Ring 3/6/9 · Thumb 0.
KPH, not WPMNumeric entry is measured as keystrokes per hour so it stays separate from prose-typing speed.
Numpad disciplineTop-row digits remain available, but advanced protocols track how much input actually comes from the numeric keypad.
