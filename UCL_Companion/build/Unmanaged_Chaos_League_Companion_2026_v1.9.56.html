Roster Needs & Moves

Health, depth & worthwhile moves
Questionable designations are treated as precautionary availability risks: the Companion can show contingency options without recommending an immediate transaction. Doubtful, Out, IR/reserve-type designations can create an actionable health need and waive the normal projection threshold. Ordinary upgrades require QB +10%; RB/WR/TE/K/DEF +20%; TE over a WR in FLEX +40%. Exact waiver-clear timing is not inferred.
